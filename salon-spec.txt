Salon Management SaaS – Feature 
Specification
Nov 17, 2026  ·  @Vinay Kumar Singh
1. Overview
This is a multi-tenant SaaS web app (with a mobile-friendly PWA). Many salon owners
each sign up, run one or more shops, and handle billing, staff commission, customers,
promotions and earnings in one place.
Goals
Bill a customer in under 30 seconds at the counter.
Calculate each worker's services, revenue and commission automatically, with no
spreadsheets.
Build a customer database with contact details and visit history, and use it for
WhatsApp/SMS promotions.
Give owners a daily, monthly and per-shop view of sales, expenses and net earnings.
Target users: small and mid-size salons, barber shops, unisex parlours and spas with 1–20
shops per owner, starting in India (INR, GST, WhatsApp-first).
In scope (MVP): shops, staff, services, billing, commission, customers, promotions,
reports.
Later phases: online appointment booking, inventory, memberships and packages, a
customer app, and payroll.
2. User roles and permissions
There are five roles, and access is scoped by tenant (owner account) and then by shop.
Implement this with spatie/laravel-permission using team = shop.

Permission rules
Deleting or editing a bill after it is saved needs the Manager role or above, plus a
reason, and it is written to the audit log.
The owner can switch between shops or see a combined "All shops" view.
Each permission is a toggle, so the owner can customise roles (e.g. allow a cashier to
give a discount up to 10%).
3. Module 1 — Tenant and multi-shop management
An owner signs up once, then adds as many shops as their plan allows and switches
between them from a header dropdown.
Features
1. Sign-up and onboarding: mobile number + OTP or email + password, then business
name, then an onboarding wizard. The wizard covers the first shop, services from a
default template, the first staff members and the bill format.
2. Shop CRUD: name, address, GSTIN (optional), phone, logo, opening hours, weekly off,
invoice prefix (e.g. HYD1- ), currency and time zone.
3. Shop switcher: a global selector stored in the session or Vuex; every API call sends X-
Shop-Id . An "All shops" option is for owners only, in reports.
4. Subscription plans: Free / Basic / Pro, limited by number of shops, staff and monthly
message credits. Use Razorpay subscriptions, with a trial period (e.g. 14 days).

4. Module 2 — Service and product catalog
The catalog is the price list the bill picks from. It is defined once per tenant, and each shop
can override the price.
Features
1. Service categories: Hair, Beard, Facial, Spa, Colour, Bridal and so on, with drag-to-sort
ordering.
2. Service: name, category, base price, duration (minutes), gender (male / female /
unisex), GST %, active flag and an optional per-service commission override.
3. Shop-level price override: the same service can cost ₹ 150 in one shop and ₹ 200 in
another.
4. Retail products (optional in MVP): name, SKU, MRP, selling price, stock quantity, and
commission on sale. Stock is reduced when a product is billed.
5. Combo / package: a bundle of services at a fixed price (e.g. Haircut + Beard + Head
massage = ₹ 349).
6. Default templates: a ready list of 40–50 common salon services to load during
onboarding.
7. Bulk import/export with CSV.
5. Module 3 — Staff, work tracking and commission
Every bill line is tagged to the worker who did it. That one link is what drives the count of
cuts, revenue per worker, and commission.
A user from tenant A can never read or write tenant B data (covered by automated
tests).
Invoice numbers are sequential per shop and per financial year, with no gaps on
concurrent billing.
Shop limits from the plan are enforced at the API, not only in the UI.

Settings choose whether commission is calculated before or after discount and
before or after GST. The default is after discount and before GST.
The rule and rate are saved on each bill line at billing time, so changing a rule later
never rewrites old earnings.
Payouts
A payout statement for any period: services done, gross revenue, commission, tips,
advances taken, deductions, fixed salary, and net payable.
Record advances and deductions (e.g. ₹ 2,000 advance on the 10th).
Mark a period as paid (cash / UPI / bank), which locks that period.
Export the statement as a PDF and share it on WhatsApp.
Staff self-view: a mobile screen showing today's count, this month's earnings, and
pending payout.
6. Module 4 — Billing / POS
The billing screen is the most-used screen, so it must work on a tablet, take few taps, and
survive a weak internet connection.
Billing flow
1. Search for the customer by mobile number. If none is found, quick-add them with
name + mobile and nothing else.
2. Add services or products from category tiles or by search; each line defaults to the
shop price.
3. Assign a worker to each line (a staff chip picker). The last-used worker is
remembered.
4. Apply a discount: per line or on the whole bill, as % or ₹ , or with a coupon code, within
the role's discount limit.
5. Tax is calculated automatically (GST inclusive or exclusive; CGST/SGST split shown
when a GSTIN is set).
6. Take payment: Cash, UPI, Card, Wallet/credit, or a split payment (e.g. ₹ 300 cash +
₹ 200 UPI).
7. Save, then print (58/80 mm thermal or A4) or send the invoice as a WhatsApp/SMS
link.

7. Module 5 — Customer management (CRM)
Customers belong to the tenant, so a customer who visits any of the owner's shops has
one profile and one history.
Customer profile
Name, mobile (unique per tenant), alternate number, email, gender, date of birth,
anniversary, address/area, notes (e.g. allergic to ammonia colour) and preferred
stylist.
Tags: VIP, Regular, Bridal, Student, or custom tags.
Source: walk-in, Instagram, referral or Google.
Marketing consent flags for SMS, WhatsApp and email, with the date captured, plus
opt-out handling.
Auto-computed fields
First visit, last visit, total visits, total spent, average bill, favourite service, and due
balance.
A 3-service bill with a known customer can be completed in 30 seconds or less.
Bill totals = sum(lines) − discount + tax, rounded per setting, with no float errors
(store paise as integers or use DECIMAL(12,2)).
A saved bill is immutable; edits create a revision and an audit-log entry.

12. Technical architecture
The stack is Laravel 11 (API) + Vue 3 SPA with Vuetify 3 + MySQL 8, with Redis queues for
anything slow (messages, PDFs, report aggregates).
All clients use one REST API. Message sending and webhooks never block a bill, because
they run in queue workers.
Key decisions
Multi-tenancy: a single database with tenant_id  + a BelongsToTenant  trait and
global scope (or the stancl/tenancy package in single-DB mode). This is simpler to run
than one database per tenant.
System architecture · 3 clients, 1 API, 4 stores/workers, 3 providers

Front end: Vue 3 + Vuetify 3, with Pinia (or Vuex 4 if your team prefers) and vue-

Billing numbers: a shop_counters  row locked with SELECT … FOR UPDATE  inside the

Commission: a CommissionCalculator  service called when a bill is saved, which
writes invoice_item_staff  with a rule snapshot; monthly slab true-up runs as a
scheduled job.
PDF / print: barryvdh/laravel-dompdf for A4; an 80 mm HTML template for thermal
printers.
Excel export: maatwebsite/excel.
Scheduler: birthday and win-back automations, nightly aggregates, due reminders,
and backups.
Hosting: a VPS or AWS Lightsail/EC2 + RDS MySQL to start; Laravel Forge or Ploi for
deploys.
